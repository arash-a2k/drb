3. Action Plan for the Content Manager (Content & GEO)
Definition Lead Architecture: Start every primary service section (H2) with a self-contained, highly concise sentence that defines the topic factually, without subjective marketing fluff. AI overviews frequently cite from the first 30% of content, looking for definitions.

Quick Answer (TL;DR) Blocks: Place a 2 to 3-sentence micro-answer immediately below main headings to directly address conversational queries in a friendly, empathetic tone.

Structured Pricing Data: Do not hide treatment costs. Create tables detailing 1405 (2026) costs for specific brands like IPS Emax, Zirconia, and Lumineers, and prominently feature installment plan (اقساطی) details. AI search agents rely heavily on structured, numeric data to answer user cost queries.

Comparative Tables: AI models aggressively seek structured comparison tables when users ask for alternatives. Create detailed tables comparing materials (e.g., Composite vs. Ceramic) and brands (e.g., IPS e.max vs. Lumineers), breaking down durability, tooth preparation requirements, and aesthetic qualities.

Content Freshness Protocol: Review and update content quarterly. Replace stale pricing or citations, update the "Last Modified" schema date, and add new patient FAQs. Un-updated pages lose AI citations at three times the normal rate.
